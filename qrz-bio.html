DE BA4RKS，一名计算机科学与技术专业学生，很高兴能同你通联！

HF段我目前使用设备有IC-718/G90S/SunSDR2 DX，天馈目前有三个选择：一根5.6m 鞭状拉杆天线搭配自制的手动调谐器，架设在家中顶楼阳台；一支自制的正V多频段适用天线，野架时使用；一支多波段适用的便携三单元八木，使用伸缩金属件作为主梁和振子，一个7米高重型灯架为支撑杆，拉多股风绳架设。通过调试，目前的设备可以满足我80米~10米波段的通联需求。

鉴于环境和设备所限，HF段目前我日常主要使用FT8/FT4模式进行通联，传播条件允许时会使用CW和SSB。CW方面我还是新手：对于听抄苦手，但是在努力练习；发报稳定在22-25wpm。我喜欢追逐异常传播的时刻，有时也会在VHF/UHF上进行数据模式通联的实验。偶尔我会以操作者的身份参加学校、俱乐部集体电台的活动。

暂不接受未实际通联的Cyber QSO；通联记录优先上传LoTW，请注意同步。

中国内地及港澳台来卡无需附回信券或其他邮资，我将在收到卡片后尽快回卡。

DE BA4RKS,a student major in Computer Science. It's a pleasure to make a QSO with you.

My interest in radio probably originated from my childhood when my father used a large-diameter parabolic antenna in the countryside to receive satellite TV channels and a homemade whip antenna at home in the city to receive analog modulated TV signals. For various reasons, I did not inherit the technology from my father. It was not until I actively participated in the Chinese Amateur Radio License A exam held in Shanghai in November 2024 that I officially started my ham career. After more than half a year (in accordance with the regulations), I successfully passed the exam and obtained the Chinese Amateur Radio License B in Wuxi, Jiangsu in July 2025, and was then permitted to communicate on the HF band. In March 2026, I obtained my FCC Amateur Extra Class license.

For the HF band, the equipments I am currently using are IC-718,G90S and SunSDR2 DX, and the antenna system has three opinions:

  • a whip(5.6m rod antenna+MTU，for 80m~10m bands), which is erected on the balcony at home;
  • a V multi-band antenna, which is used for fast field deployment;
  • a portable 3ELE Yagi suitable for multiple bands, using telescopic metal parts as the main beam and antenna elements, a 7-meter-high heavy-duty light stand as the support pole, and pulling multi-strand wind ropes to erect it.

Due to limitations in the environment and equipment, I currently mainly use the FT8/FT4 modes for communication on the HF band in my daily operations. When propagation conditions permit, I will use CW and SSB. I am still a novice in terms of CW: I struggle with copy reception, but I am practicing hard; my sending speed is stably at 22-25 wpm. I enjoy chasing moments of anomalous propagation, and sometimes I also conduct experiments on DATA mode communication on VHF/UHF. Occasionally I will participate in collective radio activities in schools and clubs as an operator.

You are very welcome to send me physical QSL cards directly. If you plan to send a direct QSL card, please reach out to me by email prior to shipping to my P.O. Box address. I will send a card back as soon as possible after receiving the card.

I will definitely reply to every card I receive, and there is no need to enclose an international reply coupon or other postage with your card.

Alternatively, you can pay me a 1 US Dollar international regular mail postage fee through the OQRS function on ClubLog (if registered mail is needed, please make an additional donation of 2 US Dollars). I will confirm with you via email as soon as possible, and then I will send the card directly to you.

I accept bureau cards as the local bureau has resumed services. Please note that processing may take several months and completion on schedule is not guaranteed.
